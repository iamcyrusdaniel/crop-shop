crop shop

how to play



controls


drag and drop crops onto the grid, once you place all your crops, press submit and you win!



crops


currently there are 3 crops in the game, being the tomato, the carrot, and the watermelon, each crop takes up a different amount of space and has a different shape, the tomato is a 1x1, the carrot is a 1x2, and the watermelon is a 2x2
